# Frozen partition table and manifest

**Rule.** `firmware/partitions.csv`, the manifest address (`OTA_MANIFEST_URL`) and its three keys (`version`, `firmware_url`, `firmware_sha256`) never change.

**Why.** Founder rule. A device cannot rewrite its partition table or bootloader over the air, and a unit unplugged for a year reads exactly that address and those keys: changing any of them strands the units in the field.

**How.**
- The manifest is generated by CI from the release (`scripts/make-manifest.py`), never committed.
- New fields may be added to the manifest; a device ignores what it does not know.
