# Pictures show the firmware as it is

**Rule.** Screen captures are taken in Demo mode and read before they are committed; a mockup shows only what the firmware does today, and a changed screen means new captures.

**Why.** Founder rule: no real account, printer name, address or serial may appear in a picture.

**How.**
- The method, the generator and how to add a view: `docs/MOCKUPS.md`, `scripts/make-mockups.py`.
- BIGTREETECH's pictures and the printer makers' logos are theirs (`TRADEMARK.md`).
