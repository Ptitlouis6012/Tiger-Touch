[Final cover](../../assets/social/pandatouch-reforged.png), a portrait editorial illustration for the [Reddit launch draft](pandatouch-reforged-reddit.md).

- [Finished device with Bambu X1 Carbon Demo mode screen](../../assets/mockups/front/panda-touch-front-printer-bambu-x1c.png)

The forge is a metaphor for replacement firmware. The screen is an illustrative reproduction of the supplied Demo mode capture, not a pixel-exact capture or proof of a live printer test. For interface evidence, use the original mockup alongside the cover. No physical modification of the screen is implied. The original assets are unchanged.

## Production prompt

Use case: ads-marketing / illustration-story.
Create one finished editorial illustration for a Reddit maker-community post, portrait 4:5, high-resolution, visually striking and readable in a mobile feed.
Campaign: "PandaTouch Reforged". This is a community-developed free replacement FIRMWARE for an existing BIGTREETECH Panda Touch, called TigerTouch, built by TigerSquad from TigerSystem. It is not new hardware. Audience: owners of Bambu Lab 3D printers who may have a Panda Touch sitting unused in a drawer.

References:
1. panda-touch-front-printer-bambu-x1c.png is the exact FINISHED device and real Demo mode UI to reproduce faithfully: black rounded rectangular front, dark gray casing, wide landscape 5-inch display, BIGTREETECH wordmark below display; X1 Carbon name, cyan 50% progress ring, AMS filament colors on right. Preserve this screen composition and readable labels instead of inventing interface elements. It is a reference/supporting insert, not a background to preserve.
2. panda-touch-front.png is the physical hardware silhouette reference for the SAME device before transformation.
3. tigersystem-icon-framed.png is the TigerSystem tiger-head brand reference; use it small and faithfully near the credit, do not invent a mascot.

Visual narrative: an idle Panda Touch coming out of a dusty open cardboard box and being reforged by makers into the exact same hardware running TigerTouch. A bold illustrated maker workshop, graphite and charcoal background, warm orange forge sparks tracing a clear left-to-right transformation. Left lower area: the SAME black Panda Touch switched off, half out of a slightly dusty cardboard box, cool muted lighting. Center/background: one compact anvil and a maker's gloved hand holding a hammer above a glowing orange circuit-shaped piece of metal, SYMBOLIZING firmware being reforged. The hammer must not strike a screen or imply that users physically modify hardware. Right/central foreground: the clean finished Panda Touch from reference 1, prominent and large enough that its actual UI can be understood, almost front-on with slight perspective, intact black housing and cyan progress ring, warm rim light. A recognizable Bambu-style enclosed printer can be a very subdued workshop silhouette, never the main subject.

Style: premium hand-crafted illustrated poster, crisp painted contours, tactile cardboard and brushed metal, cinematic contrast, confident maker zine / workshop aesthetic. Not a generic glossy corporate banner, not a meme with excessive text, not photorealistic evidence of a physical forge operation. Keep the hardware recognizable. Minimal orange circuit sparks connect the stages.
Composition: large uncluttered typographic title at the TOP; scene occupies middle 65%; concise branding and product truth at the bottom with clear spacing. High contrast typography. Finished device is the hero; box is smaller.
Exact text only, with no added slogans:
TOP two lines: "PANDATOUCH" then "REFORGED" (REFORGED in vivid orange, PandaTouch in warm white).
Small line below title: "Same screen. New fire."
BOTTOM brand: "TigerTouch" (Tiger white, Touch orange).
Under brand: "FREE, OPEN-SOURCE FIRMWARE"
Credit: "Built by TigerSquad • TigerSystem"
Small but legible footer: "Concept illustration • Screen shown in Demo mode"
Use exact spelling of PandaTouch, TigerTouch, TigerSquad, TigerSystem.
No claims that every Bambu model works, no claims of official Bambu endorsement, no pricing sticker, no QR code, no web address, no extra logos, no watermark. Do not add unsupported controls, camera feed, or fictional UI.
