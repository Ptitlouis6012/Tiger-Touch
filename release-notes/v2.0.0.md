The first public Tiger Touch: your BIGTREETECH Panda Touch or K-Touch becomes one live screen for every printer of your TigerTag account - Bambu Lab, Creality, Elegoo, Anycubic, Snapmaker, FlashForge, Prusa, Klipper and OctoPrint - installed from your browser in two minutes and kept up to date over Wi-Fi.

## Added

- Install from your browser: open the installer page in Chrome or Edge, plug the screen in, press Install. No soldering, no tools.
- Updates over Wi-Fi: the screen finds new versions by itself and installs them with two touches. Each one is checked before it is used, and a version that fails to start is undone automatically.
- A simple first start: choose your language, then your Wi-Fi, then link your TigerTag account with your e-mail or with Google by scanning a QR code with your phone. Anything can wait for later.
- Your printers come from your account: add them once in Tiger Studio Manager and they appear on the screen, with nothing to type.
- Anycubic printers through the Anycubic cloud, and Bambu Lab printers through the Bambu cloud or on your network.
- Klipper printers (Voron, Qidi, Sovol, RatRig...) and printers behind OctoPrint.
- Two views on the home screen: large live cards, or a compact list. One touch switches between them, and the screen remembers your choice.
- Pause, resume, stop and the printer's light, from the screen. Stop needs a 1.5-second hold, so a stray touch never ends a print.
- Eleven languages, and your time zone from your country, with nothing to set.

## Changed

- The screen starts in seconds and connects to Wi-Fi and to your account in the background, with a small animation while it connects.
- Scrolling is about two and a half times smoother.
- More printers can be followed at the same time: up to ten, all live.
- The connection to printers on your network is much faster and steadier.
- The settings look and work like TigerScale's, with new Screen and Language icons.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where printers of one brand could disappear when that brand did not answer.
- Fixed an issue where the same printer could appear twice.
- Fixed an issue where a printer that could not be reached showed old temperatures instead of "--".
- Fixed an issue where some printers set up on a custom port could not be reached.
- Fixed an issue where the screen could flash briefly while scrolling.

**Coming from Tiger Touch 1.x?** Install this version once over USB from the installer page: version 2.0 changes how the screen's memory is organised so that updates can come over Wi-Fi. Your Wi-Fi and account are kept.
