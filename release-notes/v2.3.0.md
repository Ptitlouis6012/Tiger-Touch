Tiger Touch 2.3.0 puts the printers that finish first at the top of the list, lets the screen stay on longer, and switches both lights of the Bambu Lab H2 series.

## Changed

- In the list view, the printers are now in the order their prints end: the one that finishes first is at the top, and printers that are not printing come after. The list holds still for a few seconds after you touch it, so a row never moves under your finger.
- The screen can now wait longer before it goes to sleep: 1 minute, 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or never. If you had chosen 30 seconds or 5 minutes, the screen now uses the next longer choice.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the light button of a Bambu Lab H2S, H2D, H2D Pro, H2C or X2D switched only one of its two chamber lights. Both now switch together.
