Tiger Touch 2.2.0 lets you pause, resume and stop an Anycubic print, and switch its light, through the Anycubic cloud.

## Added

- Pause, resume and stop now work for an Anycubic printer followed through the Anycubic cloud, as they already did on your local network. Stop still asks for a confirmation first.
- The light of an Anycubic printer followed through the cloud now switches on and off from the screen.

To use them, update Tiger Studio Manager and open it once: it adds what the screen needs to your Anycubic printers in your TigerSystem account. Until then the buttons stay dimmed.

## Changed

- The website now speaks the eleven languages of the screen on every page, has a media kit for the press, and lets you share any page.
