Tiger Touch 2.4.0 gives the printers' screens a cleaner look: the progress is a line, and the buttons are bigger and easier to reach.

## Changed

- On the printer cards and the full view of a printer, the progress is now a line instead of a ring, and the light, pause and stop buttons take the whole width of the card, right under the printer's name.
- What a printer is doing is written in colour: blue while it prints, amber when paused, green when done, red on an error or when the printer does not answer. The progress line takes the same colour, and a printer in error gently pulses.
- The green dot beside each printer is gone: a printer that does not answer shows no values, which already says it.
- The website, the README and the media kit show the new screens.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the nozzle and bed icons were missing from the list view.
