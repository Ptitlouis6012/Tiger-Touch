Tiger Touch 2.1 shows the time and says each print's state in colour. It asks before it stops a print. It also lets Tiger Studio Manager see your screen live and take screenshots of it.

## Added

- LAN mode (Settings > LAN mode): Tiger Studio Manager, on the same Wi-Fi, can show your screen live, touch it, and take a screenshot of it for a bug report or a post. It is protected by an access code shown on the screen and saved to your TigerTag account. You can draw a new code with one touch, or switch live view off.
- The time, in the middle of the top line of every screen.
- Demo mode (Settings > Demo mode), for a shop, a fair or screenshots: the screen shows ten made-up printers of every brand, printing, exactly as it shows real ones. While it is on, none of your printers is contacted.
- Settings > Battery: say whether a battery is fitted. It is on by default, since the Panda Touch and the K-Touch ship with one.

## Changed

- A print's colour now tells its state: blue while it prints, orange when paused, green when finished, red on a fault.
- Stop now asks for a confirmation before it ends a print, instead of a 1.5-second hold.
- Every screen has the same header, so nothing moves when you go from one screen to another.
- Settings shows your account's name instead of its e-mail address.
- The installer page is clearer on a computer, a tablet and a phone, and offers a light or dark theme.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the title of an AMS HT ran over the edge of its card on a printer's full view.
