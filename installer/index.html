Tiger Touch

    Install Tiger Touch on a BIGTREETECH Panda Touch or K-Touch, from this page.

    Before you start: your printers

    Tiger Touch shows the printers of your TigerTag account. Create the free account and add your printers with the desktop app Tiger Studio Manager (Windows, macOS, Linux) first: it finds them on your network, and the screen then reads them from your account, with nothing to type. The web version cannot add printers.

    Download Tiger Studio Manager (desktop)

    Firmware

    1. Plug the screen into this computer with its USB-C cable.
    2. Press Install and pick the serial port (a "USB-SERIAL CH340" entry).
    3. In the window that opens, which is in English, choose Install Tiger Touch. Leave Erase device unticked, then press Next.
    4. Wait for it to finish. The board restarts by itself.
    This browser cannot talk to a serial port. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer; Safari, Firefox and phones do not support WebSerial.
    Your saved Wi-Fi and account are kept. Ticking Erase device wipes them and gives a first start, in English. No driver to hunt for on a recent Windows; on an older one or a Mac, install the CH340 driver if no port appears.

    One screen, every brand

    Tiger Touch speaks each maker's own protocol - Bambu Lab, Creality, Elegoo, Anycubic, Snapmaker, FlashForge, Prusa - plus Klipper and OctoPrint. The stock firmwares each know one family.
