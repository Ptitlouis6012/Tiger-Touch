# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient — especially with people whose first language is not
  the one the thread is in
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Assuming good faith

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Anything a reasonable professional would consider inappropriate

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take
appropriate corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful — including editing or removing contributions,
and banning contributors temporarily or permanently.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/3Qv5TSqnJH) or by opening a private security
advisory on this repository. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the
reporter.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public.
